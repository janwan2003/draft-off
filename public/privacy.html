Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026

What we store

For each draft: its topic, settings, the suggestion list, the picks, and for each person who joins the name they type, whether they draft or vote, and their vote. That is all the service needs to work. We do not ask for an email, a phone number or an account.

Your device keeps a random key in its local storage so the site remembers you in that draft. On our side we keep only a one-way hash of that key. Anyone with a draft's link can see its picks and the names of the people in it, so don't use your full name if you'd rather not.

To stop abuse we count requests per network address for one hour, using a one-way hash of the address. The counts are deleted after about two hours.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking cookies, no analytics scripts, no selling or sharing data. Cloudflare hosts the site and may keep standard server logs.

How long

The host can delete a draft at any time. Every draft is deleted automatically 180 days after it was created. Voters can leave a draft, which deletes their name and vote.

Contact

Questions or a removal request: open an issue at github.com/janwan2003/draft-off.

About

Draftoff is a free snake-draft game for group chats.
