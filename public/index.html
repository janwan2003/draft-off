FAQ

Does everyone need to be online at the same time?

No. Each drafter gets a time per pick (5 minutes to 12 hours, or no limit). When it's your turn, open the link and pick. If time runs out, the next suggestion on the list is picked for you, so nobody holds up the chat.

What is a snake draft?

Drafters pick in order in round 1, then in reverse order in round 2, and so on. Whoever picks last in one round picks first in the next, which keeps it fair.

Who decides the winner?

Your group. After the last pick, everyone who opened the link votes for the best team (drafters can't vote for themselves) and, optionally, the single best pick. Votes stay hidden until voting closes, 24 hours after the last pick, or earlier if the host reveals them.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Everyone joins with a name. Your device remembers you, and "Private link" lets you continue on another phone.

Is it free?

Yes, with no ads. Drafts are deleted automatically after 180 days. See the privacy policy.
